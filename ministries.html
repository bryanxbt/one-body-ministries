Our Ministries

Bringing practical help and sharing the hope of Jesus Christ in Indianapolis and beyond.

At One Body Ministries we believe that meeting real needs opens doors for the Gospel, and that the body of Christ is built up through the faithful teaching of Scripture, prayer, and discipleship of every age. Every ministry is done in the name of Jesus, with the goal that people would come to know Him personally and that the people, the pastor, the preacher, and every believing member would be strengthened.

Food Pantry

We provide groceries and essentials to families and individuals in our community. Hunger is met with both provision and the love of Christ. Our building signs simply say “Meet Needs” — because that is what we are called to do.

Homeless Outreach

We go to those without shelter, offering practical help, dignity, and the hope of the Gospel. Jesus said whatever we do for the least of these, we do for Him. We aim to be His hands and feet on the streets of Indianapolis.

Men’s & Women’s Bible Study

Separate studies for men and women where we open the Word of God together. We seek to grow in sound doctrine, accountability, and practical obedience so that believers are equipped for every good work.

Prayer Groups

We gather to pray for one another, for the church, for our city, and for the advancement of the Gospel. Prayer is not an afterthought — it is the foundation of all we do as the body of Christ.

Youth Ministry

We invest in the next generation with biblical teaching, discipleship, and genuine community. Young people are not the church of tomorrow only — they are part of the body of Christ today and need to be rooted in truth.

Children’s Ministry & Sunday School

We teach children the Word of God in age-appropriate ways so they may know Jesus from their earliest years. Sunday School and children’s ministry are places where the next generation hears the Gospel clearly and sees it lived out.
