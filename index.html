A Word from Pastor Chris Dunn

One Body Ministries exists for one reason: to serve Jesus Christ by serving others. Whether you are in need of practical help, looking for a place to grow in your faith, or sensing a call to serve alongside us, you are welcome here.

We believe the local body of Christ is called to love in action — meeting real needs while pointing every person to a living relationship with the Lord Jesus.
