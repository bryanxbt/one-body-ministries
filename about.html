About One Body Ministries

Serving Jesus Christ by serving others — so that people may know Him personally.

Our Mission

One Body Ministries is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We exist to serve Jesus Christ by serving others in the community and throughout Indianapolis. Our heart is to help people build a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

We believe the body of Christ is called to practical love: meeting real needs while faithfully pointing every person to the hope found only in the Lord Jesus. We do not compromise on the truth of Scripture or on our calling to support the people, the pastor, the preacher, and every believing member of the body of Christ.

One Body

The name “One Body” comes from the clear teaching of Scripture: “For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ” (1 Corinthians 12:12). We are not a collection of isolated individuals. We are members of one another, called to care for each other and to serve the world in Jesus’ name.

Pastor Chris Dunn

Pastor Chris Dunn leads One Body Ministries with a pastoral heart for both the practical needs of the community and the spiritual growth of believers. Under his leadership the ministry seeks to be a place where the Gospel is lived out in word and deed — bringing help and sharing hope.

What Guides Us

Everything we do is measured against two questions:

  • Does this strengthen faith and practical life in Christ for the people, the pastor, the preacher, and the believing members of the body?
  • Is it faithful to Scripture without compromise?

If the answer to either is no, we do not pursue it. Our design, our words, and our work are meant to serve the church, not the culture’s trends.
